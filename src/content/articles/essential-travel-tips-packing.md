There is nothing quite like the feeling of walking straight off an airplane, bypassing the crowded baggage claim, and heading straight to your destination. Packing carry-on only is a travel superpower.

Here is the exact strategy to pack light without leaving the essentials behind.

## The Packing Strategy

### 1. The 5-4-3-2-1 Rule
For a week-long trip, use this basic count:
* **5** Pairs of socks and underwear.
* **4** Tops (t-shirts, shirts, or blouses).
* **3** Bottoms (pants, shorts, or skirts).
* **2** Pairs of shoes (one worn, one packed).
* **1** Hat or jacket.

### 2. Roll, Don't Fold
Rolling your clothes saves substantial space and reduces wrinkles. Roll items tightly and pack them side-by-side.

### 3. Use Compression Packing Cubes
Compression cubes use extra zippers to squeeze air out of your clothes, shrinking the volume by up to 30%. They also keep your suitcase organized.

### 4. Wear Your Bulkiest Items on the Plane
Save space and weight by wearing your heaviest boots, thickest jacket, and hat during transit.
